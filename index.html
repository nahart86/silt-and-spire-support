center of mass
A tiny physics lesson

Every sculpture has a tipping point.

Silt & Spire models each grain as it lands and settles. Moisture changes grain size and cohesion; the shape above the base shifts the load. Keep the center of mass supported, or watch gravity take over.

There is no timer and no score to chase. The pleasure is in noticing what a small adjustment does.
