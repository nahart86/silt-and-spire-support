A closer look

See each pour take shape.

Watch a stream gather into small ridges and rising towers. The mixture changes how the sand spreads, holds, or slips away.

The pleasure is in the pause

One drop changes the shape.

Use Add a pinch for a short pour, or hold and drag to guide a continuous stream. The sculpture rises one small deposit at a time, and each one can change its balance.

01

Set your foundation

Begin with the generous Cove, find your footing on Spire, or take on the narrow Ledge.

02

Feel the mixture

Move the slider from dry, loose sand through a damp mix to runny sand that settles into softer shapes.

03

Read the balance

Follow the center of mass as the sculpture grows. A tall pile can lean, buckle, or topple when its support runs out.

center of mass
A little balance goes a long way

Every sculpture has a tipping point.

See each shape form in three dimensions. As it grows, its weight shifts: a wide base can hold more, while a narrow neck or leaning top may tumble. Find out how the mixture changes what stands and what slides.

There is no timer or countdown. Local best heights and foundation goals give each attempt a mark to beat.
