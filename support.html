Help & FAQ

Silt & Spire is in development. The notes below describe the intended game experience; App Store availability has not yet been confirmed.

Getting started

How do I add sand?

Tap the play area to place one grain. To add a steady stream, press and hold while dragging to guide the drop across the foundation. Release to stop.

What does the moisture slider change?

It changes the sand mixture. Drier sand is more crumbly; wetter sand is heavier and more fluid. The grains settle differently as moisture changes. The control is a standard slider with an adjustable value as well as touch input.

What are Cove, Spire, and Ledge?

They are three foundations with different widths. Cove is broad, Spire is narrower, and Ledge is the most constrained. Each gives the same sand a different balance problem.

Why did my sculpture lean or fall?

The game tracks the sculpture’s center of mass and support. A pile can become unstable when its weight shifts beyond the ground contact or a narrow section can no longer bear the load. A topple is part of the physics, not a lost online score.

How do I start again?

Use Restart to clear the active pile and begin a fresh attempt on the selected foundation. Your saved postcards and best local records are kept when you restart a pile. A collapse ends the current build; restart to try another arrangement.

Saved sculptures and sharing

Where are my saved sculptures and records?

They are stored on this iPhone in the app’s local storage. There is no account, server, cloud sync, or remote leaderboard. You can save a sculpture as a postcard and view or remove postcards from your shelf.

How does PNG sharing work?

Choose to export a postcard and iOS opens its share sheet. You choose whether to share and which destination to use. A destination you select may handle the image under its own privacy terms.

Will my saved work come back if I delete the app or change phones?

Saved work is kept locally and is not synced or backed up by Silt & Spire. Deleting the app removes its local data. We do not provide a restore service or guarantee that app data will transfer to another device.

Accessibility and controls

Can I play without dragging?

Yes. Tapping places a single grain, so you do not need to hold and drag. The moisture level can be adjusted with the slider control as well as by touch. Controls are designed to work with iOS accessibility features, including VoiceOver labels and adjustable slider values.

Are the controls small?

We are designing the controls for clear labels and comfortable touch targets. If a control is difficult to use on your device, email us with the device model and iOS version so we can investigate.
