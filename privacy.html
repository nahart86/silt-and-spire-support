Privacy

Silt & Spire is designed to work offline. It does not collect personal information or send game data to us.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Information handled by the app

The app does not ask for an account and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases, third-party SDKs, or network connection. We do not receive your gameplay, device identifiers, usage data, or contact details through the app.

Your active sculpture, saved postcards, and best records are stored locally on your iPhone. They are not uploaded or synced by Silt & Spire. App data may be included in device backups according to your Apple device settings; that backup is controlled by Apple, not by the developer.

Sharing a postcard

When you choose to export a PNG, iOS presents its share sheet. You decide whether to share the image and choose a destination. That destination may collect or process information according to its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive a copy through Silt & Spire.

Storage and deletion

Saved sculptures remain on the device while the app and its data remain installed. You can remove saved postcards in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to any device backup you manage. Silt & Spire has no cloud restore or cross-device sync service, so we cannot restore locally saved work.

Support website and email

This is a separate static support website. Visiting it may involve technical request data handled by the website hosting provider and your browser under their policies. The game itself does not send your activity here. If you email support, Nathan Hart receives the message and the contact details you choose to include so he can respond. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Children

The app has no account, ads, social features, or collection of personal information. If you have a question about a child’s privacy, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this notice

If app behavior or this notice changes, this page will be updated with a new revision date. The App Store privacy information will be kept aligned with the released app.
